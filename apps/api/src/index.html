Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about the platform and autonomous agency operations.

How does NeedVane surf for content ideas on its own? ▼

NeedVane crawls public discussion channels across YouTube, TikTok, and Reddit. It uses a 14-parameter parser to detect unresolved friction, repeatedly asked questions, and negative sentiment toward existing solutions. It then calculates an empirical multi-factor viability score and surfaces the top 3 topic recommendations.

Can I set different revenue split formulas for different creators? ▼

Yes. The Storefront module allows you to generate unique creator URLs for two, three, or more creators for the exact same product. For Creator A you can configure a 70/30 split at \$67, while for Creator B you can configure a 65/35 split at \$97. Each creator gets their own access link and transparent sales accounting.

What is the 35–45 Page High-Density Standard? ▼

Unlike shallow 5-page summaries, every digital asset produced by NeedVane includes 10 fully articulated chapters, implementation frameworks, action step companions, prompt vaults, and worksheets. Each asset undergoes a 10-stage verifiable quality rubric assessment before passing QA.

How do I track creator email responses and interest? ▼

The Creator Outreach dashboard features a live Inbound Response Inbox with AI sentiment analysis. Incoming replies are categorized into Interested (🔥), In Discussion (💬), or Declined (❌). You can immediately approve interested creators and generate their unique partner link with a single click.
