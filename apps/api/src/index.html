Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about the autonomous agency pipeline.

How does the system discover market pain points without guesswork? ▼

NeedVane crawls public discussions across YouTube, TikTok, Amazon reviews, and Reddit. It analyzes recurring customer complaints, tests them against the 5 Survival-Level Pain criteria, verifies that contemporary digital stores have no direct solutions, and outputs the top 5 high-probability opportunities.

Can I customize the revenue sharing formula per creator? ▼

Yes. Every creator partner can have their own unique storefront tracking URL, customized split formula (such as 70% Creator / 30% Us), and transparent live sales accounting.

What is the 35–45 Page High-Density Standard? ▼

Every digital asset engineered in Product Studio includes 10 articulated chapters, a Chapter 1 "Quick Win" action, workable tools (checklists and templates), and an 80/20 long-term maintenance protocol—ensuring publication quality with zero generic fluff.
